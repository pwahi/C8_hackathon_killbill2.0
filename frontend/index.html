Paid more than you expected?

Kill Bill reads your bill, checks every sum, shows exactly where the difference comes from, and writes a polite message you can send to the company.

How to use it

  1. Your bill. Upload a PDF or photos and say what you expected to pay.
  2. Read. The AI reads every price. You choose which pages it sees.
  3. Check the lines. Compare the lines with your bill and fix anything wrong.
  4. What we found. See where any difference comes from, checked by code.
  5. Your message. Copy the ready-made message and send it yourself.

What the AI does, and what the code does

  1. YouYour bill and the amount you expected
  2. AI · Claude HaikuReads the prices and how the bill is grouped
  3. Code · PythonChecks every sum and finds the difference
  4. AI · GPT-OSSSuggests why, and words the message
  5. Code · PythonFills in every amount and checks the wording
  6. YouCheck the message and send it yourself

The AI only reads and writes words. Every number comes from code.

What happens to your data

  • Sent to the AI: only the pages you tick, when you press Read.
  • Saved: the checked lines, the result and your message, private to this browser.
  • Never saved: your bill image or PDF.
  • Never done: Kill Bill does not contact the company or send anything for you.
